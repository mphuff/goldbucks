# Changelog

The website shows the newest entries from this file. Each heading is a version; the lines under it are what changed.

## 0.4.0

- First release under the name Goldbucks.
- Goldbucks only runs in worlds where its game rule is on (Create World > More > Game Rules, or `/goldbucks on`). All your other worlds stay normal Minecraft.
- The town: a town square with a Town Board of paid orders, townsfolk who visit shops, a Town Store with house upgrades, pets and more, a morning newspaper, levels and perks, achievements, a Mayor and elections, and 21 town events.
- New jobs: Builder, Farmer and Banker.
- The side bar shows each player's cash and net worth.
- A settings screen for the host, which checks every change and refuses settings that would break the game.
- Houses are built on dry land, and the town has more wheat and other supplies to go round.
